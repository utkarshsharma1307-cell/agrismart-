Available on every page in AgriProtect

Control AgriProtect with Your Voice

Navigate, ask questions, and manage your farm - all without typing. Works in English and Hindi.

  • Floating Widget: Click the green "Assistant" button at bottom-right of any page
  • Voice Button: Click the microphone icon inside the assistant widget
  • Full Assistant: Visit the dedicated Assistant page for the command center experience

    How to Use

    1. Open the Assistant

      Click the green "Assistant" button at bottom-right of any authenticated page

    2. Click Microphone

      Inside the widget, click the microphone icon to activate voice input

    3. Allow Permission

      Your browser will ask for microphone access. Click "Allow" (required once per browser)

    4. Speak Clearly

      Say your command or question in English or Hindi

    5. Get Response

      The assistant navigates, answers, or executes your command

    Customize Voice Settings

    🎛️Available in Settings Page

  • Enable/Disable: Turn voice on or off completely
  • Speed: Adjust from 0.5x to 2x (default 0.9x)
  • Pitch: Change from 0.5x to 2x (default 1x)
  • Test Voice: Preview with sample audio

    Tips for Best Results

  • Speak clearly at normal pace
  • Use simple commands ("soil", "weather")
  • Complete sentences for questions
  • One language per command
  • Minimize background noise
  • Check browser permissions if not working
  • Adjust speed in Settings if too fast/slow
    • 🔒 Privacy: Voice processing happens locally in your browser using Web Speech API. No audio is recorded or sent to servers. Responses are rule-based and local - no external AI services used.
    ℹ️ Demo Data: Weather, market prices, and recommendations are demo values. Always confirm with local experts and current information before making farm decisions.
